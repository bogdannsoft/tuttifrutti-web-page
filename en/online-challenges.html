How to play · Turn-based

Online Challenges

Play with people anywhere, anytime. In online challenges, each player answers whenever it's their turn — no need to be online at the same time.

Create a challengePlay your turnCustom categoriesAchievements

Create or find a challenge

You can tap "Play" to jump into an automatically matched challenge, or go to the online challenges section to choose how you play. From there you can also see the challenges you created, the ones you're playing, and the finished ones.

  1. Tap create. Set up players, categories, time per round, and number of rounds.
  2. Choose participants. Invite random players, favorites, or available friends, within your level's limit.
  3. Confirm and start. Tap "Play" and get ready to fill the categories with the chosen letter.

Your turn, your pace

Fill in words as fast as you can and tap the red paper to call stop. The fastest stop in the round sets the cutoff time: answers written after that moment don't count.

Once you're done, your challenge waits for the other players. They'll find it by searching challenges or receive it automatically when it's their turn. Once everyone completes their round, the results and winners show up.

Custom categories and corrections

You can also create challenges with categories you make up yourselves. Add at least one custom category when creating the challenge, and once everyone's finished playing, an extra review stage kicks in.

In that stage, you all review the custom words and vote. If negative votes make up more than half the players, the word is worth 0 points. Correct, unique answers score 10; repeated ones score 5. Each player must submit their corrections before the challenge expires.

If a challenge expires: it wraps up with whatever results are available at that point, and winners are determined from there.

Play for the trophies

TuttiFrutti runs weekly and monthly tournaments. Players who score the most points in official categories get a trophy at the start of each period. You can check your achievements from your profile.

Choose who to play with

From the challenge creation screen you can invite favorites and friends. To add someone as a favorite, open their profile and pick the corresponding option; they'll then be available for future matches.
